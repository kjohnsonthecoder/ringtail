Plain English in, rules that must fire out.

Describe a behavior or paste a threat report. Claude drafts a Sigma rule with test samples; Ringtail's code validates it against pinned MITRE ATT&CK v20.1 and replays the samples. It must fire on the attack and stay quiet on benign activity, or it goes back for another try.
